Status: source inventory for WikiGnome expansion. This is **not** a set of published film articles or invented canon. Credits are transcribed from the existing poster artwork; uncertain or absent credits are not supplied. No release dates are assigned. The films are real **within Gnomeville**, not real-world productions.

## WikiGnome editorial rules agreed with Management

1. Each film receives a WikiGnome film entry, using the poster as the authoritative source for credited names, including repeated Milo Chicken credits.
2. Milo, Paw-Paw, and all other explicitly credited people/characters should have linked filmography or crew credits on their existing wiki entries where appropriate.
3. Do not invent additional cast or crew. Fictional production anecdotes may be created, clearly distinguishable from poster-derived credits.
4. Do not assign release years or dates. Preserve *Claw II* as Coming Soon.
5. Keep Gnomeflix (streaming service) separate from Gnomeville Pictures (production studio).
6. The real-world origin: Management made the posters for fun after Toni printed an earlier Scarface-themed Milo image. This is out-of-universe background, not in-universe film history.
7. Film posters should be hosted in GitHub, not hotlinked from Drive.
