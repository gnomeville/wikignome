# WikiGnome Publishing and Asset Provenance Rule

**Status:** Approved standing rule (2026-11-14).

## Required publication workflow

1. Google Drive remains the working archive and source-of-truth for preserved original materials.
2. Every image, PDF, document, transcript, illustration, scan, ephemera item, and other media asset **published in WikiGnome** must have a copy stored inside the `gnomeville/wikignome` GitHub repository.
3. WikiGnome article markup must reference its own repository-hosted assets by relative paths (for example, `assets/exhibits/naskerman-affair/...`), **not direct Google Drive URLs**. External websites may be cited as sources when appropriate, but they must not substitute for hosting published WikiGnome assets.
4. Before publication, verify that the asset is present in the repository and that its article link resolves. Keep originals and editorial masters in Drive; copying to GitHub must not delete, move, or alter them.
5. Organize published files in descriptive folders under `assets/`, preserving meaningful filenames and source provenance. Where helpful, include an exhibit/source note in the article.
6. If an asset cannot be copied or permission to publish is uncertain, do **not** publish a Drive hotlink as a workaround. Mark the item pending and report the blocker.
7. For previously published WikiGnome pages, audit any remaining Drive-linked media and migrate them to the repository before claiming full compliance.
